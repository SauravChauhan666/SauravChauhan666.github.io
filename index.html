About

I'm a full-stack developer at BitQit, working on Sparq — a Shopify search and merchandising platform. Day to day that means a NestJS / TypeORM / PostgreSQL backend on one side and a Vue.js / Nuxt dashboard on the other, plus the Shopify Admin API, webhooks and OAuth flows holding the two together.

I graduated in May 2026 with a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence & Data Science from GGSIPU, which is where the other half of my work comes from — computer vision, PyTorch, and LLM integration. It's a useful combination: I can build the product and the model behind it.

What I'm best at is the underspecified problem. Give me a vague bug report or an undocumented migration path, and I'll investigate it across services and come back with the fix plus the migration steps and documentation the team needs to actually run it.
